Close your tabs. Keep your place.

Earmark is a pause button for your browser. It saves a whole window, how far you'd read on every page, and a note about what's next. When you come back, one click puts everything back exactly where you left off.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store See pricing

A Chrome extension. Free to use, with an optional one-time Pro upgrade.

How it works

Three steps, no setup, no account.

  1. Pause

    Click Earmark or press Alt + Shift + P. Your window is saved and its tabs close, so you can stop with a clear head.

  2. Leave a note

    Type or dictate what you were doing and what comes next. Notes are dated, so you can see how the work moved along.

  3. Resume

    Later, click Resume. Your tabs come back in order, each one scrolled to where you were, with your note waiting.

Saves your place, not just your links

Most tab managers keep a list of addresses. Earmark keeps the reading position and the thinking around it.

Reading positions

Earmark finds your spot by the text you were reading, so long articles, docs and threads open at the right paragraph.

Notes that keep their dates

Each time you come back, a new dated entry starts. Older entries fold away until you want them.

Categories and @mentions

Group related sessions, and mention one session in another's notes to jump between them in a click. @mentions are part of Pro.

Notes on single pages Pro

Leave a note on one tab. It shows up on that page when you resume, and stays out of sight otherwise.

A safety net

Closed a window without pausing? Earmark keeps it for a while, so you can still get it back.

Search everything

Find any session by its name, notes, page titles or addresses. Rename, file and delete in a few keystrokes.

Your sessions never leave your computer

Earmark has no servers and no account. What you save is stored in your browser, and only you can see it.

  • No tracking, analytics or ads
  • Never reads what you type into websites
  • Private windows are never saved
  • Export or delete everything at any time

Questions

What happens when I reach 20 sessions on the free version?

Pausing still works. Your least-used session moves to Recently deleted, where you can bring it back for 30 days. Nothing is ever locked: free or Pro, you can always open, resume and export every session.

Can Earmark see my browsing?

No. Earmark runs entirely in your browser and has no servers. It only saves the windows you pause, and it never reads what you type into websites.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. You pay $9.99 once and Pro is yours. There's nothing to renew or cancel.

Can I use Pro on more than one computer?

Yes. One license key works in up to 5 browsers at once, for example your home and work computers. You can free up a spot from Earmark's settings at any time.

What if Earmark isn't right for me?

Email us within 14 days of buying Pro and we'll refund you in full. See the refund policy.

Which browsers does it work in?

Earmark is built for Google Chrome on Windows, Mac, Linux and ChromeOS.
